# Custom Corrugated Boxes

## Intro
PackJoyU supplies custom corrugated boxes for shipping, e-commerce and retail. Tell us what you need to pack and how many, and our team will propose a structure, material and print options, then confirm everything in a written quote.

## Use cases
- E-commerce mailer boxes
- Shipping cartons for distribution
- Shelf-ready and display boxes for retail

## Materials and options
- **Styles:** Mailer (tuck-front) boxes, regular slotted cartons, die-cut boxes, display boxes
- **Board:** Kraft or white corrugated board; flute and wall chosen per product

## Customization and printing
Flexo, digital or litho-laminated printing, outside and inside the box; varnish, lamination and inserts can be discussed.

## From inquiry to delivery
1. **Send your brief** — product, size, quantity and artwork or a reference.
2. **Quote** — we propose structure, material and printing; prices and terms are confirmed in writing.
3. **Proof and sample** — digital proof first, then a physical sample if needed.
4. **Production run and quality checks** — after you approve the sample, we arrange the production run and quality checks.
5. **Packing and shipping** — shipping terms and destinations are confirmed in the quote.
