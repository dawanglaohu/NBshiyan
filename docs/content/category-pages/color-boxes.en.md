# Custom Printed Boxes

## Intro
PackJoyU makes custom printed folding cartons for retail products. Tell us what you need to pack and how many, and our team will propose a structure, material and print options, then confirm everything in a written quote.

## Use cases
- Cosmetics and personal care
- Food and confectionery retail packs
- Electronics and accessories

## Materials and options
- **Styles:** Straight or reverse tuck-end, auto-lock bottom, sleeve, window, hanger tab
- **Board:** White or kraft paperboard; grammage per product

## Customization and printing
Offset CMYK and Pantone spot colours; foil stamping, embossing, spot UV and lamination can be discussed.

## From inquiry to delivery
1. **Send your brief** — product, size, quantity and artwork or a reference.
2. **Quote** — we propose structure, material and printing; prices and terms are confirmed in writing.
3. **Proof and sample** — digital proof first, then a physical sample if needed.
4. **Production run and quality checks** — after you approve the sample, we arrange the production run and quality checks.
5. **Packing and shipping** — shipping terms and destinations are confirmed in the quote.
