# Custom Wine Boxes

## Intro
PackJoyU makes custom wine boxes for wineries, importers and gift sets. Tell us what you need to pack and how many, and our team will propose a structure, material and print options, then confirm everything in a written quote.

## Use cases
- Single-bottle gift boxes
- Multi-bottle and tasting sets
- Shipping boxes for wine

## Materials and options
- **Styles:** Magnetic flap, lid and base, book-style, tube, corrugated shipper
- **Materials:** Rigid greyboard with specialty paper; corrugated board for shipping

## Customization and printing
Offset printing; foil, embossing, spot UV, soft-touch and ribbon can be discussed.

## From inquiry to delivery
1. **Send your brief** — product, size, quantity and artwork or a reference.
2. **Quote** — we propose structure, material and printing; prices and terms are confirmed in writing.
3. **Proof and sample** — digital proof first, then a physical sample if needed.
4. **Production run and quality checks** — after you approve the sample, we arrange the production run and quality checks.
5. **Packing and shipping** — shipping terms and destinations are confirmed in the quote.
