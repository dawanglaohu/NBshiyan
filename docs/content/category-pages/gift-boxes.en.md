# Custom Gift Boxes

## Intro
PackJoyU makes custom rigid gift boxes for brands and corporate gifting. Tell us what you need to pack and how many, and our team will propose a structure, material and print options, then confirm everything in a written quote.

## Use cases
- Premium product packaging
- Corporate and seasonal gift sets
- Subscription and PR boxes

## Customization and printing
Offset printing on the wrapping paper; foil, embossing, spot UV, ribbon pulls and inserts can be discussed.

## From inquiry to delivery
1. **Send your brief** — product, size, quantity and artwork or a reference.
2. **Quote** — we propose structure, material and printing; prices and terms are confirmed in writing.
3. **Proof and sample** — digital proof first, then a physical sample if needed.
4. **Production and inspection** — production starts after you approve the sample.
5. **Packing and shipping** — shipping terms and destinations are confirmed in the quote.
