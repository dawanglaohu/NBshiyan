### H1: Custom Printed Boxes

PackJoyU sources and supplies full-color printed paperboard boxes for cosmetics, electronics, food, supplements and retail products. From tuck-end folding cartons to boxes with windows and inserts, each design is printed with accurate brand colors and finished with foil, embossing or coating to stand out on the shelf. We work to your dielines or develop a new structure with you.

#### Why PackJoyU

- **Shelf impact** — Offset CMYK + Pantone printing for vivid, consistent brand color.
- **Premium finishes** — Hot foil, embossing/debossing, spot UV, soft-touch or gloss lamination.
- **Structures shaped to your product** — Tuck-end, auto-bottom, sleeve, window and hanger-tab boxes.
- **Proof before production** — Digital and physical proofs so you approve color and fit first.

#### Specifications & options

| Option | Details |
|---|---|
| Styles | Straight/reverse tuck-end, auto-lock bottom, sleeve, window, hanger tab |
| Material | White or kraft paperboard (e.g. SBS, C1S, grey-back); grammage per product |
| Printing | Offset CMYK, Pantone spot colors |
| Finishes | Foil stamping, embossing, spot UV, matte/gloss/soft-touch lamination, window patching |
| Sizes | Custom |
| MOQ | Confirmed with your quote |
| Sample lead time | Timing confirmed with your quote |
| Production lead time | Timing confirmed with your quote |

#### How it works

1. **Send your brief** — product size, quantity, artwork or reference.
2. **Quote & structure** — we propose material, structure and dieline.
3. **Proof & sample** — digital proof, then physical sample (timing confirmed with your quote).
4. **Production & QC** — mass production with inspection before packing.
5. **Shipping** — packing and shipping documents; terms confirmed per quote.

- **Заметность на полке** — Офсетная печать CMYK и Pantone — яркий и стабильный фирменный цвет.

- **Конструкция под ваш товар** — Коробки с клапанами, с автодном, футляры-слайдеры, с окном и европодвесом.
- **Проба до тиража** — Цифровая и физическая проба — вы утверждаете цвет и посадку заранее.
